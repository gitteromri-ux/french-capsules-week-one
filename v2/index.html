How the numbers are counted

Registrations count capsules, not orders: one order can include 1, 2 or 3 capsules. Each order is counted from its contract value at the website prices (3 monthly payments): 1 capsule $297, 2 capsules $534, 3 capsules $567; with the 20% CAPSULE20 code $237, $426 and $453.

Revenue is the full 3-month contract value of all 12 orders from 11 buyers. Spend is media spend for the Culture Capsules campaigns from October 21, 2026, capped at $15,000, reached partway through November 14 ($98.29 of that day's $748.57). Israel time. ROAS = revenue ÷ spend, media only.
