Reporting basis & definitions

All amounts are USD and reflect the agreed reporting scope for October 24 to November 1, 2026. Media spend includes only the two French Capsules sales-objective campaigns, excluding language-course lead generation.

Acquisitions represent four Meta-attributed purchases. Registrations count the capsules in the price-matched packages, not CRM order rows. Package mix and full contract value are reconstructed from individual reported purchase amounts and published checkout pricing: three $151 monthly bundles with $453 contracts, and one $79 monthly single with a $237 contract.

ROI = (full contract value − media spend) ÷ media spend. It excludes other business costs and is not realized cash profit. Initial collection is the first-payment value reported to Meta, not an independently reconciled bank-settlement total. Remaining contract value is not yet collected.
