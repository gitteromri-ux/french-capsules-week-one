How registrations are counted. One purchase can include 1, 2 or 3 capsules, so registrations are counted from contract value, not from CRM order rows. Live website prices, checked November 24: 1 capsule $297 ($99 × 3), 2 capsules $534 ($178 × 3), 3 capsules $567 ($189 × 3). With the 20% CAPSULE20 code: 1 capsule $237 ($79 × 3), 2 capsules $426 ($142 × 3), 3 capsules $453 ($151 × 3). The CRM total is $2.29k for 6 acquisitions. The Week 1 four (3 × $453 + 1 × $237 = $1,596) leave $690 for the two new buyers, which only $453 + $237 matches. Total $2,286. That gives 4 × 3 + 2 × 1 = 14 registrations, or 2.33 capsules per buyer. Meta’s first-payment values confirm it: four of $151 and two of $79.
