How the numbers are counted

Buyers and contract value: 6 acquisitions and $2.29k contract value are the CRM totals. The 6 purchases above add up to $2,286.

Purchase details: the date, hour, campaign, ad and first payment of each purchase come from Meta purchase data for the two Capsules sales campaigns (Israel time).

Capsules (registrations): one purchase can include 1, 2 or 3 capsules, so registrations count capsules, not orders. Each first payment matches exactly one bundle on the live website prices, checked November 24: 1 capsule $99/month ($297), 2 capsules $178 ($534), 3 capsules $189 ($567). With the 20% CAPSULE20 code: 1 capsule $79 ($237), 2 capsules $142 ($426), 3 capsules $151 ($453). So $151 is all three capsules with CAPSULE20, and $79 is one capsule with CAPSULE20.

Paid at checkout is the sum of the first monthly payments, not a bank-settlement total.
