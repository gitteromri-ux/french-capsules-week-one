How the numbers are counted

Sales: the French Atelier Daily Report in Sisense (Cultural Capsule courses): the eCommerce row has 10 acquisitions and $4.35k contract value; Malek’s sale ($453) is counted as e-commerce with approval. 7 of the 10 e-commerce buyers are attributed in Meta, with the date, hour, campaign, ad and month-1 value shown above; the other 3 are organic (in the BI eCommerce row, not attributed in Meta).

Capsules (registrations): the BI counts one registration per order, so it undercounts buyers who take more than one capsule. Capsules are counted from each buyer’s contract value, using the website prices (3 monthly payments): 1 capsule $297, 2 capsules $534, 3 capsules $567; with the 20% CAPSULE20 code $237, $426 and $453. The 7 Meta buyers total $2,853. The 3 organic buyers make up the rest of the $4.35k, and $426 + $534 + $534 = $1,494 is the only combination of website prices that fits, so the e-commerce total is $4,347 (BI shows $4.35k). Malek’s $453 is already the full contract value, which is 3 capsules with CAPSULE20.

Real revenue vs Meta value: Meta receives only the first monthly payment, so its $951 covers month 1 of the 7 attributed purchases. Real revenue is the full 3-month contract value of all 11 buyers: $4,800.

Spend is the Meta total for campaigns #116939 and #116980 from October 22 to November 16, 2026, Israel time. ROAS = revenue ÷ spend, media only.
