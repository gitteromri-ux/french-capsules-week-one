Reporting basis & calculations

All amounts are USD and reflect the agreed reporting scope for October 24 to November 1, 2026. Marketing spend includes only the two French Capsules sales-objective campaigns, not language-course lead generation.

Acquisitions represent four Meta-attributed purchases. Registrations count the capsules in the price-matched packages, not CRM order rows. Package mix and full contract value are reconstructed from the reported purchase amounts and the published checkout pricing: three $151 monthly bundles with $453 contracts, and one $79 monthly single with a $237 contract.

ROI = (contract value − marketing spend) ÷ marketing spend. This media-only calculation excludes other business costs and is not realized cash profit. Collection is the initial-payment value reported to Meta, not an independently reconciled bank-settlement total. Remaining contract value is not money already collected.
